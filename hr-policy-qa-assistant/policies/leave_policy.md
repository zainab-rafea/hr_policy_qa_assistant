# Leave Policy

## Section 1: Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
prorated for employees who join mid-year. Leave requests must be submitted at least
5 working days in advance via the HR portal and require manager approval. Unused
annual leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar
year; any excess is forfeited unless local law requires otherwise.

## Section 2: Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 14 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is
required for absences of 3 or more consecutive days. Sick leave does not require
advance notice, but employees must notify their manager before the start of their
shift, or as soon as reasonably possible.

## Section 3: Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 90 calendar days of paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are entitled to 14 calendar days of paid parental leave.
Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption date.
Employees must notify HR at least 30 days before the expected leave start date.

## Section 4: Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an
immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling), and up to 2 paid
days for extended family members.

## Section 5: Unpaid Leave
Employees with over 1 year of tenure may request unpaid leave of up to 30 days
for personal reasons, subject to manager and HR approval. Unpaid leave beyond
30 days requires VP-level sign-off and may affect benefits continuity.
