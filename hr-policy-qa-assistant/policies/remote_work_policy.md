# Remote Work Policy

## Section 1: Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 90-day probation period and hold roles
classified as "remote-eligible" by their department head may work remotely.
Roles requiring on-site equipment, lab access, or in-person customer contact
are not eligible.

## Section 2: Hybrid Schedule
Hybrid employees are required to be on-site a minimum of 2 days per week,
coordinated with their team lead. Core collaboration hours are 10:00-16:00
local time, during which employees must be reachable regardless of location.

## Section 3: Fully Remote Arrangements
Fully remote arrangements require director-level approval and are reviewed
annually. Employees working fully remote from a different country than their
contracting entity must obtain approval from HR and Legal at least 60 days
in advance, due to tax and employment law implications.

## Section 4: Equipment and Expenses
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office stipend of $500 for
remote and hybrid employees. Internet reimbursement of up to $50/month is
available upon submission of a receipt.

## Section 5: Data Security
Remote employees must use company-issued VPN software when accessing internal
systems and may not use public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks to access confidential
data. Violations may result in revocation of remote work privileges.
